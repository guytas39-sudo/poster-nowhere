A travel poster for **Velmara**, a place that isn't there: an island where
the tide comes in twice a day and the lighthouse keeps the hours. One tall
banded lighthouse on a rocky point, two moons in a dusk sky — a full gold
moon high up and a small rose crescent low over the headland — and a lone
rowing boat with a lantern crossing the light-path on the water.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib + PIL, no network) draws the poster and
writes `out/entry.png` — 1024×1024, deterministic: two runs produce
byte-identical output.
